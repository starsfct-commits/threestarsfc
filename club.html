Our story

Work hard, play for each other and make the community proud.

Three Stars Football Club is a community football club from Imbali, Pietermaritzburg, playing in the Hollywoodbets Regional League under the South African Football Association.

Since at least 2015 the club has brought players, coaches and supporters together around that one idea. Home games are played at Imbali Unit 1, Endishini, where entry is free and the whole community is welcome.

You’ll know us by the sky-blue shirts and the three gold stars. And after most weekends you’ll see the same line on the club group: another 3 points in the bag.

Along the way

Club timeline.

What the club’s own posts tell us so far. If you have older photos or results, send them through the Facebook group.

  1. 2015

    Awards evening and a home online

    The club’s Facebook group went up on 22 June 2015, and the same day it filled with photos from an awards evening: a table of trophies, certificates and plenty of proud players.

  2. 2025

    Regional league football at Endishini

    The year opened with a 2–2 draw against Midlands Academy at Imbali Unit 1, Sarh scoring both. Big away wins followed: 6–1 at Phatheni FC and 4–0 at City United.

  3. 2026

    Back-to-back October wins

    A 4–1 home win over Masthokoze FC on 18 October, then a 2–0 clean sheet at Ashdown Stadium a week later.
